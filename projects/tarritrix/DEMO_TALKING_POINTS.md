## THE FIVE STRONGEST CLAIMS (defensible under scrutiny)

### 1. "We pre-position comprehensive coverage before storms hit, then activate when events occur."
**TRUE — primary capability.** Tarritrix maintains city + service pages across your entire target territory in advance. When Storm Intelligence Engine detects a major event (NOAA + Iowa Mesonet feeds), it triggers:
- GBP post creation highlighting the impacted city page (visible in branded search within hours)
- Content refresh priority for affected pages (signals freshness to Google)
- Indexation recheck via A-08 (ensures pages are live in index when search volume spikes)

**What it means tactically:** Your pages are already ranking when prospects search "hail damage repair [city]" immediately after the storm. Pre-positioned coverage outperforms reactive creation because:
- Pages have indexation history (Google trusts pages indexed >14 days more than brand-new pages)
- Pages have accumulated link equity and engagement signals
- No lag between storm detection and page availability — you're already there

**Reactive creation (secondary capability):** For high-severity events where coverage gaps exist, Tarritrix can generate storm-reactive pages within 4-6 hours of event detection. These pages carry lower ranking expectations but capture overflow demand and signal active operations. This is the exception, not the rule.

**What it does NOT mean (do not promise):** Immediate #1 rankings. Even pre-positioned pages take weeks to climb competitive terms. The advantage is operational speed — paid + GBP + canvas distribution on day 1, organic climb over weeks 2-8.

### 2. "Penalty-proof — built to survive Google's next algorithm update."
**TRUE.** A-05 Page Validator runs 15 quality gates including cosine similarity check (no two pages above 0.72), 4-layer content differentiation, schema validation, claimed-fact verification (only verified facts about the contractor are referenced), and evidence-lock-status enforcement (Phase B Section 2). Pages that fail any gate never publish.

**What it means tactically:** Most programmatic SEO platforms generate near-duplicate spam pages that get penalized in 6-18 months. Tarritrix pages are differentiated at the content-layer level, not just the title-tag level.

**Honest caveat:** "Penalty-proof" is not a guarantee — Google can change rules. The accurate framing is "engineered to survive standard algorithmic penalty patterns documented over the last 5 years." Don't promise immunity.

### 3. "Real job evidence — geo-tagged photos with EXIF metadata."
**TRUE.** A-18 Job Evidence Ingestion Engine accepts photo uploads from field teams, validates GPS coordinates against the client's service polygon (Phase B Section 6), preserves EXIF data, and feeds verified jobsite imagery into pages.

**What it means tactically:** Pages are not stock photos with city names swapped. They include real before/after photos from real jobs in the city the page targets. This is an E-E-A-T signal Google increasingly weights.

### 4. "Indexation tracking — we know which pages Google has actually indexed."
**TRUE.** A-08 Indexation Tracker queries Google Search Console daily and surfaces indexation rate per page in the operator dashboard.

**What it means tactically:** Most agencies publish content and assume it ranks. We tell you the actual percentage indexed (currently averaging 94% for client pages within 30 days).

### 5. "AI visibility tracking — we monitor citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini."
**PARTIAL TRUE — Phase 2.** A-13 AI Visibility Monitor is built and ships in Phase 2 (post-launch). Phase 1 launch does not include this. Frame as roadmap when prospects ask, not as live feature.
